How Website Analyzer Works

Our 5-stage progressive analysis system completes in approximately 30 seconds, giving you instant insights while performing deep technical audits in the background.

Enter URL & Instant Checks

2 seconds

Enter your website URL and we immediately check HTTPS, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, SSL certificate, favicon, mobile viewport, and response time. This gives you quick health indicators in under 2 seconds.

SEO Analysis

5 seconds

We analyze your meta tags, heading structure (H1-H6), Open Graph tags, Twitter Cards, canonical tags, image alt text, schema markup, and internal linking structure. This comprehensive SEO audit identifies optimization opportunities.

Performance Metrics

8 seconds

Using Google PageSpeed Insights API, we measure Core Web Vitals including Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), First Contentful Paint (FCP), Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), and Time to Interactive (TTI). These metrics directly impact your Google rankings.

Design Quality Analysis

10 seconds

Our advanced computer vision system analyzes your website screenshot to evaluate design quality, color harmony, typography, user experience, and mobile responsiveness. You get 3-5 specific issues and actionable recommendations based on modern web design best practices.

Personalized Improvement Plan

8 seconds

Finally, our algorithm generates a custom 4-week roadmap prioritized into Critical Issues (Week 1), Growth Opportunities (Week 2-3), and Advanced Enhancements (Week 4). Each item includes problem, solution, impact analysis, and investment estimates.

Total analysis time: Approximately 30 seconds for complete results
