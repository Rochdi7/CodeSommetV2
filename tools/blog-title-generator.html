Blog Title Best Practices

  • •Keep it 50-70 characters - Perfect for Google search results and social sharing
  • •Use power words - Words like "ultimate," "proven," "secret" increase clicks
  • •Include numbers - Odd numbers (7, 9, 13) convert better than even numbers
  • •Create curiosity - Hint at valuable information without revealing everything
  • •Match search intent - Ensure your title answers what users are searching for
