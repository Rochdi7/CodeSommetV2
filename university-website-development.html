Common Challenges

The Challenge: Universities & Higher Education Businesses Face Unique Website Challenges

Generic web developers don't understand these universities & higher education-specific pain points

Outdated University Websites

Most university websites are 10+ years old with poor UX, slow loading, and non-mobile responsive. This hurts enrollment as 70% of prospective students research on mobile devices.

Manual Paper-Based Admissions

Processing paper applications, manual document verification, and email-based communication is time-consuming. Admissions teams waste 30+ hours per week on repetitive tasks.

No Student Self-Service Portal

Students calling/emailing for transcripts, enrollment status, fee receipts, and course schedules overwhelms staff. You need a self-service portal for 80% of common queries.

Poor International Student Experience

No multilingual support, confusing visa documentation processes, and lack of international payment options deter foreign students who bring higher tuition revenue.

Low Application Conversion Rates

Complex application forms, slow response times, and poor mobile experience cause 60% of started applications to be abandoned. You're losing qualified students to competitors.

Disconnected Systems

Separate systems for admissions, LMS, finance, and student records create data silos. Staff waste hours on duplicate data entry and students face fragmented experiences.

Sound familiar? We've solved these exact problems for 40+ universities & higher education businesses.
