Common Challenges

The Challenge: EdTech & E-Learning Businesses Face Unique Website Challenges

Generic web developers don't understand these edtech & e-learning-specific pain points

Generic LMS Platforms Are Limiting

Teachable, Thinkific, and Kajabi charge 5-10% transaction fees, limit customization, and own your student data. You need your own platform to scale profitably.

High Transaction Fees Eating Profits

Paying $500-$2,000 per month in platform fees plus 5-10% per transaction is unsustainable. Custom platforms save $10,000+ annually for course creators earning $100K+.

Low Course Completion Rates

Industry average is 15% course completion. Poor UX, no gamification, and lack of engagement features cause students to abandon courses halfway through.

No White-Label Branding

Your students see "Powered by Teachable" branding and competitor ads. You need a fully branded platform that reinforces your authority and builds trust.

You Don't Own Student Data

Platform providers control your student list, email data, and analytics. If they change policies or shut down, you lose everything. You need data ownership.

Limited Integration Options

Can't connect with your existing CRM, email marketing, or custom tools. You're locked into their ecosystem with expensive add-ons.

Sound familiar? We've solved these exact problems for 40+ edtech & e-learning businesses.
