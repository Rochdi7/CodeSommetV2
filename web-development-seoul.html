Success Story

How We Built a Live Commerce Platform Generating $50M Monthly GMV

Live streaming e-commerce and social shopping platform
•
E-Commerce / Social Commerce
•
9 months

The Challenge

A Seoul-based e-commerce startup wanted to build Korea's next live shopping platform, combining TikTok-style short videos with real-time purchasing during livestreams. Requirements included HD live streaming with <500ms latency, one-click purchasing during broadcasts without leaving the stream, integration with Korean payment systems (Naver Pay, Kakao Pay, Toss), influencer/brand dashboards showing sales analytics, automated inventory sync with fulfillment partners, and Kakao Talk integration for order notifications - all while handling 50K+ concurrent viewers during popular streams.

Our Solution

We built a Next.js live commerce platform with ultra-low latency live streaming using AWS IVS optimized for Korea, implemented one-click checkout overlays allowing purchases without leaving the stream, integrated all major Korean payment methods (Naver Pay, Kakao Pay, Toss, Samsung Pay), built influencer dashboard with real-time sales, viewer engagement, and earnings tracking, created automated product recommendations using AI during livestreams, added Kakao Talk chatbot for order updates and customer service, synced inventory in real-time with Coupang Fulfillment and other 3PLs, and deployed on AWS Seoul region with CDN optimized for Korean ISPs.

Measurable Impact

$50M monthly GMV within 10 months of launch

Peak concurrent viewers: 85K during celebrity streams

Average conversion rate: 12% (industry avg: 3%)

Series B funding secured ($35M) based on growth metrics
