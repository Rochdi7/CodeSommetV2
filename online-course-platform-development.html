Common Challenges

The Challenge: Online Course Platforms Businesses Face Unique Website Challenges

Generic web developers don't understand these online course platforms-specific pain points

Platform Fees Are Killing Your Profits

Teachable takes 5% + Stripe fees. Kajabi charges $199-$399/month. If you're earning $100K+, you're paying $5,000-$10,000+ per year just to host your courses.

Limited Customization

Template-based platforms restrict your branding, design, and user experience. You can't match your brand identity or create unique course experiences.

No Control Over Student Experience

Generic course players, limited engagement features, and competitor branding hurt your authority. Students see you as "just another course on Teachable".

Platform Lock-In Risk

If Teachable/Kajabi changes pricing, shuts down, or modifies features, you're stuck. You don't own your platform or student relationships.

Poor SEO & Discoverability

Course platforms give you a subdomain (yourname.teachable.com) which hurts SEO. Custom domains with proper SEO rank 10x better on Google.

Can't Integrate Your Tools

Limited API access means you can't connect your CRM, email tools, or custom automations. You're forced into their ecosystem with expensive add-ons.
