Acceptable Use Policy

Guidelines for responsible and lawful use of Pikasso Studio's services and platform.

Permitted UsesProhibited UsesViolations

Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of Pikasso Studio's website, services, and platform. This policy is designed to protect our users, our business, and the broader internet community from irresponsible or illegal activities.

By using our services, you agree to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy. This AUP is part of our Terms of Service and must be read in conjunction with it.

IMPORTANT: Violation of this policy may result in immediate suspension or termination of your account and services without prior notice or refund.

Permitted Uses

You may use our services for lawful purposes, including but not limited to:

  • Business Websites: Creating professional websites for legitimate businesses, organizations, and personal brands
  • E-commerce: Building online stores to sell legal products and services
  • Content Publishing: Sharing blogs, portfolios, news, educational content, and creative works
  • Community Building: Creating forums, social networks, and community platforms (subject to moderation)
  • Professional Services: Promoting consulting, freelancing, agencies, and professional services
  • Non-profit Organizations: Websites for charities, NGOs, and community organizations
  • Educational Platforms: Online learning, courses, tutorials, and educational resources

We encourage innovation and creativity. If you're unsure whether your intended use is permitted, please contact us before proceeding.

Prohibited Uses

You may NOT use our services for any of the following:

1. Illegal Activities

  • Any activity that violates local, state, national, or international law
  • Promoting, facilitating, or engaging in illegal activities
  • Selling or distributing illegal drugs, weapons, counterfeit goods, or stolen property
  • Money laundering, fraud, or financial crimes
  • Human trafficking, exploitation, or illegal immigration services
  • Gambling or online betting (unless properly licensed)

2. Harmful or Abusive Content

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child exploitation
  • Content promoting violence, terrorism, or extremism
  • Hate speech, discrimination, or harassment based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
  • Doxing or sharing personal information without consent
  • Threats, intimidation, or incitement to violence
  • Self-harm, suicide promotion, or eating disorder encouragement

3. Spam & Malicious Activities

  • Sending unsolicited bulk emails (spam)
  • Phishing, scamming, or social engineering attacks
  • Distributing malware, viruses, ransomware, or other malicious software
  • Creating fake websites to impersonate others or steal credentials
  • Automated scraping or harvesting of user data without permission
  • Denial-of-service (DoS) or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks

4. Intellectual Property Violations

  • Hosting pirated software, movies, music, books, or games
  • Copyright infringement or unauthorized use of copyrighted materials
  • Trademark infringement or brand impersonation
  • Distributing cracked software, keygens, or license circumvention tools
  • Plagiarism or passing off others' work as your own

5. Service Abuse

  • Excessive bandwidth consumption or resource hogging
  • Running cryptocurrency mining operations without explicit permission
  • Hosting file-sharing or torrent sites
  • Creating multiple accounts to evade bans or restrictions
  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to our systems or other users' accounts
  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, or attempting to extract source code

6. Deceptive Practices

  • Pyramid schemes, multi-level marketing scams, or get-rich-quick schemes
  • False advertising or misleading product claims
  • Creating fake reviews, testimonials, or ratings
  • Impersonating individuals, businesses, or organizations
  • Spreading misinformation, disinformation, or fake news

7. Adult & Explicit Content (Restrictions Apply)

  • Pornographic content or adult entertainment (prohibited without prior approval)
  • Escort services or sexual content involving minors
  • Non-consensual intimate images ("revenge porn")
  • Sexually explicit content without proper age verification

Note: Legal adult content may be permitted on a case-by-case basis with proper age verification, content warnings, and compliance with all applicable laws. Contact us for approval.

8. Privacy Violations

  • Collecting personal data without consent or proper disclosure
  • Selling user data without explicit permission
  • Violating data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
  • Installing tracking software or spyware
  • Unauthorized surveillance or monitoring

Zero Tolerance Policy

We have a zero-tolerance policy for certain violations, including child exploitation, terrorism, and severe illegal activities. Such violations will result in immediate account termination and reporting to law enforcement.

Security & Responsible Use

You are responsible for:

  • Account Security: Maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials
  • Password Protection: Using strong, unique passwords and enabling two-factor authentication when available
  • Monitoring Activity: Regularly reviewing your account activity for unauthorized access
  • Timely Reporting: Immediately notifying us of any security breaches or unauthorized use
  • Compliance: Ensuring all content and activities comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Third-Party Services: Vetting and monitoring any third-party plugins, integrations, or services you use

Security Best Practices

  • Keep all software, plugins, and themes up to date
  • Use SSL/TLS certificates for encrypted connections
  • Regularly backup your website and data
  • Implement strong access controls and user permissions
  • Monitor logs for suspicious activity

Consequences of Violations

If you violate this Acceptable Use Policy, we may take one or more of the following actions:

1. Warning

Issue a formal written warning for minor, first-time violations with a requirement to remedy the issue within a specified timeframe.

2. Content Removal

Remove or disable access to specific content that violates this policy without prior notice.

3. Service Suspension

Temporarily suspend your account and services for repeated or serious violations while we investigate.

4. Account Termination

Permanently terminate your account, delete all data, and ban you from using our services. No refunds will be issued for violations.

5. Legal Action

Report violations to law enforcement, regulatory authorities, or other appropriate parties. We may cooperate fully with legal investigations and provide evidence as required.

6. Financial Liability

Hold you financially responsible for any damages, costs, legal fees, or losses incurred as a result of your violations.

Important Notes:

  • We reserve the right to take immediate action without prior notice for severe violations
  • Actions taken are at our sole discretion based on the severity and nature of the violation
  • Terminations due to policy violations are not eligible for refunds
  • We may preserve records and evidence for legal and compliance purposes

Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any content or activity that violates this Acceptable Use Policy, please report it to us immediately.

How to Report

Send a detailed report to [email protected] with the subject line: "AUP Violation Report"

Please include:

  • URL or location of the violation

  • Any other relevant details

We take all reports seriously and will investigate promptly. We may contact you for additional information. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent permitted by law.

Emergency Reports

For urgent matters involving immediate danger, child safety, or criminal activity, contact local law enforcement immediately before contacting us.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Acceptable Use Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our business practices.

We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date
  • Sending email notifications to active users
  • Displaying a prominent notice on our website

Your continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
